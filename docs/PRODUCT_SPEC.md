# TeachVero product specification

English-facing Italian teaching business for learners arriving from social media. Primary journey: campaign/home → lessons/packages → validated inquiry → persisted request → authenticated teacher review. Secondary: learn → article/quiz → guide signup; returning learner → sign-in → own requests, profile and progress.

Sitemap: /, /about, /lessons, /packages, /learn, /learn/[slug], /contact, /ciao, /login, /dashboard, /admin, /legal/[slug].

Design: ivory paper, charcoal ink, coral calls to action, lavender/mint lesson accents. Large editorial serif paired with system sans; custom vector Italian scene instead of invented teacher photography. 44px minimum controls, visible focus, reduced motion, mobile menu and bottom CTA.

Stages: 1 foundation and inquiry/admin vertical slice; 2 editorial pages/resources; 3 protected student/admin workflows; 4 tests, responsive audit and handoff. No payments, email or confirmed bookings until configured. Prices, qualifications, testimonials, business and legal details remain pending owner review.
