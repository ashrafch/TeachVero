# Brand direction for TeachVero
TeachVero is the personal brand of Veronica, an independent professional Italian-language teacher for international learners. The brand sells trust in a person, not access to a directory. Reference Preply and Superprof for usability only; never copy their layouts, motifs, wording, illustrations or branding.

## Workflow
1. Read existing brand assets, content, and design tokens. Treat unknown photos, credentials and reviews as missing data, not license to invent.
2. Produce a one-page creative brief: target learner, unique promise, three personality adjectives, visual metaphor, typography pair, 5–7 design tokens, photo direction, visual anti-patterns.
3. Propose two meaningfully different visual routes using actual TeachVero content; select a direction and note trade-offs before coding.
4. Establish a single source of truth for colors, type, radius, spacing, shadow, motion. Build distinctive art direction: editorial hero with authentic teacher portrait, original language-learning motifs, differentiated content layouts.
5. Design **mobile at 360px first** and verify 390, 768, 1024, 1440. Preserve legibility, real tap targets, and contrast.
6. Capture screenshots where available, critique composition and apply revisions. Distinguish functional pages from mere mockups.

## Quality bar
- Strong readable typographic hierarchy; no generic gradient/SaaS landing page; no random emojis as decoration.
- Authentic and welcoming, adult-appropriate, international, inclusive.
- Never invent awards, student counts or testimonials. Avoid automatic carousels or distracting motion.
- Explain design decisions in `docs/design-decisions.md` and export reusable tokens.
