# Editorial engine
Every piece must serve a learner objective and a measurable next action. Target adults learning Italian, with a warm, precise, non-patronizing teacher voice.

Create content briefs with audience level, pain point, search/social intent, learning objective, lesson outline, supporting practice, proofing checklist, CTA and repurposing angles (Reel, carousel, email, site article).

Each article should be original, with examples checked for linguistic correctness; identify when teacher approval is needed. No fabricated credentials, testimonials, guarantees or student outcomes. Attribute externally sourced material and respect licensing.

Lead magnets should provide real standalone value (e.g., mini pronunciation guide, common phrases worksheet), with optional follow-up and clear email consent. Avoid forced signup for trivial samples.

Maintain a content model with slug, locale, CEFR level, topic, owner, status, publish/update dates and related offer. Ensure accessible media captions, alt text and PDF/download usability.
